# Monster pose generation prompts

Built-in imagegen, transparent background. Each sheet references its existing approved monster frame. These prompts record intended constraints, not verified output properties.

## mantis-idle

Create a production-oriented animation POSE STUDY sheet for the EXACT monster in the reference image. Preserve its identity, armor pieces, proportions, colors, horns/antennae, chest features, hands and feet in every frame. This is pixel art matching the supplied native sprite, not polished illustration. Crisp dark outline, small deliberate square pixel clusters, stepped 3–5 tone shading, consistent light from upper left. No smooth gradients, blur, painterly rendering or new costume details.

Layout: EXACTLY 4 columns × 3 rows = 12 fully separate complete full-body poses on TRUE TRANSPARENT alpha background. Equal square cells, no drawn grid lines, no captions, no labels, no text. Each cell is a conceptual160×160 frame, intended sheet640×480 logical pixels; output may be uniformly enlarged (e.g.2048×1536) but every cell must remain identical dimensions. Character consistently faces LEFT in the same slightly three-quarter front arcade camera. Same character scale and pivot across all12 cells. Keep feet baseline at90% of EACH cell height, root centered at50% cell width. Leave safe space around horns, hands and feet; nothing crosses cells. Character pose should occupy same height as original; do not zoom per pose. Planted foot remains at same pixel on ground across poses unless specified lifted. No scenery, floor, drop shadow, motion trails, ground dust, energy projectiles or detached VFX. Only character animation poses. Use limb articulation and torso posture changes, never substitute whole-body translation alone.

Each ROW is a separate four-frame animation read LEFT TO RIGHT. Keep strong readable silhouettes and meaningful differences between adjacent frames.
ROW1 gentle breathing loop: (1) neutral combat-ready idle with arms relaxed, (2) inhale chest rises slightly and shoulders lift, (3) deepest inhale with very slight knee straighten and hands subtly opened, (4) exhale toward neutral. Feet remain planted throughout.
ROW2 roar/emote: (1) crouch slightly and tuck head, (2) raise head and open arms with elbows flexed, (3) roar with head back and torso tall, clawed hands spread, (4) settle arms and head toward neutral. For armored boss express roar through posture, DO NOT turn its helmet into a new mouth.
ROW3 heavy stomp: (1) shift weight onto rear leg, (2) raise forward knee and foot clearly off ground with arms balancing, (3) drive raised foot down to the ground baseline with torso compressed and elbows braced, (4) recoil upward and recover neutral. The support foot stays planted.
Identity specifics: green insectoid armored monster, yellow-green segmented torso, orange-red eyes, two thin antennae, magenta shoulder spikes, TWO silver sickle forearms. Keep sickles attached and symmetric in design through every pose.
